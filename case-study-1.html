CASE STUDY 01

AI-Assisted Customer Onboarding

Improving a manual customer profile and onboarding process through AI-assisted automation, structured data, quality assurance, and operational workflow improvements.

Project Overview

Customer onboarding often requires information to be reviewed, organized, and entered into a consistent structure before it can be used effectively by an operations team.

This project focused on improving that process by using AI-assisted workflows and structured data while maintaining quality assurance and human review.

Challenge

Reduce repetitive manual work while maintaining consistency and quality during customer onboarding.

Approach

Apply AI-assisted workflow design to organize information into a more structured and repeatable process.

Focus

Balance automation with quality assurance and operational review.

Process Improvement Approach

The first step was understanding the existing onboarding process and identifying where repetitive work, inconsistent formatting, and manual review created unnecessary effort.

The improved workflow was designed around structured information, AI-assisted processing, and clear review points so that automation could support the process without removing necessary oversight.

Quality Assurance

Quality assurance was an important part of the workflow design. AI-assisted output still required a process for checking that information was complete, consistent, and appropriate before it moved forward.

This helped keep the workflow focused on practical operational improvement rather than automation for its own sake.

Business Value

  • Reduced repetitive steps within the onboarding process.
  • Created a more structured approach to customer information.
  • Supported greater consistency through workflow design and QA.
  • Demonstrated how AI can support existing business operations.
  • Created a process designed to be easier to manage and scale.
