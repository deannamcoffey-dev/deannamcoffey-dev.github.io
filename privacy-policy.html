Effective Date: September 19, 2026
Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Doe Farm Solutions LLC (“Doe Farm Solutions,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal and business information responsibly.

By using our website or providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name

  • Job title
  • Information submitted through contact or consultation forms
  • Communications you send to us

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through general website contact forms unless specifically requested.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website hosting, analytics, security, or other service providers.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed

  • Approximate geographic information derived from an IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Website interaction and usage information

We may use this information to operate, secure, analyze, and improve our website and services.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Doe Farm Solutions and service providers supporting our website may use cookies and similar technologies for functions such as website operation, security, analytics, performance measurement, and user experience.

Cookies are small data files placed on or associated with your device.

Depending on your browser and the technologies used on our website, you may be able to block, limit, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

If we introduce advertising, remarketing, or additional tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those practices.
