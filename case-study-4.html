CASE STUDY 04

College Baseball Recruiting Email Automation

An automated workflow that extracts player information from recruiting emails and organizes the data in Google Sheets for faster and more consistent review.

Project Overview

College recruiting information often arrives through email and requires someone to manually review each message and transfer important player details into a spreadsheet.

This project demonstrates how business systems and data integration can connect Gmail, Make.com, Regex parsing, Google Sheets, and Apps Script to reduce repetitive data entry.

The Challenge

Recruiting information had to be manually reviewed and transferred from emails into spreadsheets.

The Solution

Automatically identify recruiting emails, extract player information, and create structured spreadsheet records.

The Value

Reduce repetitive work and create a more organized recruiting review process.

The Business Problem

Recruiting emails contain valuable player information, but much of that information is embedded inside email subject lines and message content instead of a structured database.

Manually transferring this information takes time and can lead to inconsistent formatting or missed information.

The Automation Workflow

  1. Gmail monitors incoming recruiting emails.
  2. Make.com receives relevant email data.
  3. Regex and text parsing extract recruiting information from the email.
  4. Player information is separated into individual data fields.
  5. The information is mapped to the correct Google Sheets columns.
  6. A new player record is automatically added to the recruiting spreadsheet.
  7. Apps Script can further organize records based on location or other recruiting criteria.

Recruiting Data Captured

  • Date email received
  • Class year
  • Player name
  • Position
  • Height
  • GPA
  • City
  • State
  • Recruiting video URL
  • Player profile URL

Data Organization

Google Apps Script can further organize recruiting records after they are added to the master spreadsheet.

For example, New Hampshire players can be routed to an in-state tab while players from other states can be organized into an out-of-state tab. This keeps the master data intact while making the recruiting information easier to review.

Business Value

  • Reduces repetitive recruiting data entry.
  • Creates consistent player records.
  • Makes recruiting information easier to search and sort.
  • Reduces the chance of information being missed.
  • Allows staff to spend more time reviewing players.
  • Creates a scalable workflow as email volume increases.
