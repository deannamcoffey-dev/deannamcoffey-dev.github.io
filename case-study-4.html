College Baseball Recruiting Email Automation

An automated recruiting workflow that transforms incoming college baseball recruiting emails into structured player records using Gmail, Make.com, Regex, Google Sheets, and Google Apps Script.

Recruiting Email Automation Overview

College baseball recruiting can generate a large volume of emails containing player names, graduation years, positions, school information, statistics, video links, profile links, and other recruiting details.

Reviewing each recruiting email manually and transferring the information into a structured system can be time-consuming and inconsistent. This project demonstrates how recruiting automation can capture incoming emails, extract relevant player information, organize the data in Google Sheets, and create a more efficient recruiting workflow.

Challenge

Reduce the repetitive manual work required to review recruiting emails and organize player information for recruiting staff.

Automation Solution

Use Make.com, Gmail, Regex, Google Sheets, and Apps Script to extract recruiting information and convert it into structured player records.

Operational Impact

Create a more consistent recruiting intake process that turns unstructured emails into organized player information for easier review and tracking.

Recruiting Automation Workflow

The workflow below demonstrates how incoming recruiting emails are captured, processed, and transformed into structured player information that can be stored and reviewed in Google Sheets.

How the Recruiting Automation Works

  1. Gmail monitors incoming college baseball recruiting emails.
  2. Make.com retrieves the email content for processing.
  3. Pattern matching and Regex locate recruiting information contained within the email.
  4. Player details are converted into structured data fields for easier review.
  5. Video and recruiting profile URLs are captured when present in the message.
  6. The structured player information is added to Google Sheets.
  7. Google Apps Script supports additional data processing and organization within the recruiting workflow.
  8. Gmail can send an automated response when the workflow requires a confirmation or follow-up message.

Recruiting Email Data Extraction

Recruiting emails are a form of unstructured data. Important player information may appear in different locations within the email or may be included as plain text, labels, or links.

The workflow uses pattern matching and Regex to identify information that can be consistently extracted and mapped into structured fields in Google Sheets.

  • Player name
  • Graduation year
  • Position information
  • School or team information
  • Contact or recruiting details
  • Video URLs
  • Recruiting profile URLs

Unstructured Email to Structured Data

One of the main goals of this workflow is to convert information contained in recruiting emails into a format that is easier to review, organize, and manage.

Instead of repeatedly copying and pasting player information into a spreadsheet, the workflow creates a consistent recruiting intake process that organizes the available player data automatically.

This type of workflow demonstrates how email automation and data extraction can be applied to other business processes where important information arrives through unstructured messages.

Recruiting Automation Tools & Methods

Make.com Gmail Regex Google Sheets Google Apps Script Pattern Matching Data Extraction Workflow Design Automation Testing

Automation Challenges & Problem Solving

A key challenge was working with recruiting email content that could vary from one message to another. Unlike information submitted through a standardized form, email text does not always follow the same structure.

Pattern matching and Regex were used to locate recognizable pieces of player information and extract them into structured fields where possible.

The recruiting automation also required testing with different email formats to confirm that player records remained organized and that video and recruiting profile links were captured correctly.

Operational Impact

This recruiting automation creates a more structured process for handling information received through email. It reduces repetitive data entry while making player information easier to organize and review.

  • Reduces repetitive copying and pasting from recruiting emails.
  • Creates a more consistent recruiting intake process.
  • Makes player information easier to review and compare.
  • Centralizes recruiting information in Google Sheets.
  • Improves visibility into video and recruiting profile links.
  • Converts unstructured email information into structured operational data.
  • Demonstrates how email automation can support data-heavy administrative workflows.
