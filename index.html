About Doe Farm Solutions

Practical operations experience combined with modern automation tools to help businesses work more efficiently.

Doe Farm Solutions was founded by Deanna Coffey with a simple goal: help businesses make their operations easier to manage without making technology more complicated than it needs to be.

With experience across operations management, workflow design, business systems, analytics, payroll, onboarding, and automation, the focus is always on solving the operational problem first and choosing the right technology second.

That might mean improving an existing process, connecting systems that do not communicate, automating repetitive work, organizing business data, or introducing practical AI tools where they can provide real value.

The goal is not automation for the sake of automation. It is creating simpler, more reliable operations that give business owners and teams more time to focus on the work that matters.
